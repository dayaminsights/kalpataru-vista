The deal

What's included, and the terms

Two recurring charges, and they are separate jobs: one keeps the site running, the other changes it. Neither bills for the other's work, and both start free.

Hosting & care

Keeping the site up: VPS, SSL, CDN, daily backups and uptime monitoring. Free for six months from go-live, then a monthly fee, month to month, no lock-in. Stopping it does not cost you the site.

Support & maintenance

Changing the site: content edits, new sections, fixes and new work. Free for three months from go-live, then an hourly rate, billed against the hours actually worked.

The domain

Yours. You buy it and it stays registered in your name; we point it at the site and set up the records.

Who owns the code

You do, outright, at go-live. Flat files, no platform licence, no proprietary CMS — it runs on any static host.

What you supply

Photography, floor plates, the price sheet, the payment plan and the RERA registration — the agent trains on the same material.
