What is already built

The site you've already seen — what's actually in it

Nothing in this section is a proposal. It is what the homepage does today, in the order a buyer meets it, so the prices further down are quoted against something you can open and check.

Hero
The Kalpataru logotype draws itself on in outline and then fills, with the tower render behind it. The sequence is scrubbed to the scroll rather than played on a timer, so the visitor sets the pace and can hold it anywhere.
01 — Show Apartment
A looping walkthrough of the apartment that is built and open to visit. It does not start downloading until it scrolls into view, so it never competes with the hero for bandwidth, and it swaps to a play control for anyone who has asked for reduced motion.
02 — Overview
Three cards. Sector 128, where the map gives way to a location film on hover. The golf course, cycling through its stills. And the residences, which opens the floor-plan request.
03 — Amenities
A panel that pins to the screen and steps through three groups as the visitor scrolls — play, work, rest — each with its own images and its own list of what sits inside it.
04 — Unit Layout Plan
The 3 and 4 BHK side by side: super area, usable area, price from. Under them, a call-back plate that takes a name, a number, a time to call and which configuration they are weighing up.
05 — Site Layout & Specifications
The master plan, and beside it the specification schedule in six groups: complex and building, apartment, kitchen, bathroom, security and safety, terrace.
06 — Why Kalpataru Vista
The four practices credited on the project, each swapping the photograph beside it when the visitor picks it, then the group's record counted up as it comes into view, and the four benefits that close the section.
07 — FAQs
Answers grouped by subject — the residences, location and connectivity, price and approvals, developer and design, visiting and downloads — filtered live as the visitor types, with a form for anything not covered.
Enquiry close
Book a site visit, download the plans, the address, the phone number, the email, and the RERA registration.

The parts that are not a section

  • Two lead forms — the floor-plan download and the call-back request — each collecting name, number and intent, then opening a pre-filled email to the sales team.
  • A sticky enquire bar on the phone that appears once the hero is behind the visitor and stands down again when the enquiry section is on screen.
  • Meta description, Open Graph and Twitter cards, so a link pasted into WhatsApp unfurls with the render and the copy rather than a bare URL.
  • The RERA registration in the footer and on the enquiry section.
  • The show-apartment video and the counters respect a reduced-motion preference, and every card that reacts to a mouse reacts to the keyboard too.
  • The body face is self-hosted rather than fetched from a font service, and the site runs as flat files, so it can sit on any static host.

Not in this build: a sitemap, and any handoff from the enquiry forms into a CRM — today they open an email. Both sit in the scope priced further down.
