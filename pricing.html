06 — Why Kalpataru Vista

The credits, the record, the close

Four practices are credited on the project — HB Design on architecture, Studio HBA on interiors, Burega Farnell on landscape, DJ Coalition on lighting — and each one swaps the photograph beside it when the visitor picks it. Under them the group's record counts up as it comes into view, and four benefits close the section.

The parts that don't have a picture

  • 01 — Show Apartment. A looping walkthrough of the apartment that is built and open to visit. It does not start downloading until it scrolls into view, so it never competes with the hero for bandwidth, and it swaps to a play control for anyone who has asked for reduced motion.
  • 07 — FAQs. Answers grouped by subject — the residences, location and connectivity, price and approvals, developer and design, visiting and downloads — filtered live as the visitor types, with a form for anything not covered.
  • The enquiry close. Book a site visit, download the plans, the address, the phone number, the email, and the RERA registration.
  • Two lead forms — the floor-plan download and the call-back request — each collecting name, number and intent, then opening a pre-filled email to the sales team.
  • A sticky enquire bar on the phone that appears once the hero is behind the visitor and stands down again when the enquiry section is on screen.
  • Link previews. Meta description, Open Graph and Twitter cards, so a link pasted into WhatsApp unfurls with the render and the copy rather than a bare URL.
  • Accessibility and restraint. The show-apartment video and the counters respect a reduced-motion preference, and every card that reacts to a mouse reacts to the keyboard too.
  • No dependencies to fail. The body face is self-hosted rather than fetched from a font service, and the site runs as flat files, so it can sit on any static host.

Not in this build: a sitemap, and any handoff from the enquiry forms into a CRM — today they open an email. Both sit in the scope priced further down.

A full-service agency really does do more — brand strategy, photography, campaign management, an account team to call on a Sunday. What sits between them is senior craft at a price that is not carrying an agency's floor space.

The optional agent

An AI that talks to your buyers

A chat agent trained on your own price sheet, floor plates and RERA filing — answering at three in the morning, and handing the sales team a qualified lead before anyone has opened a laptop.

The case for it is two figures already on this page. The industry answers a property enquiry in 9172 minutes on average, and only 9%2 of brokerages reply inside the five-minute window, while the buyer is still on the page and still comparing. Neither number is really about staffing. Nobody is at a desk at three in the morning, and a buyer browsing at three in the morning does not wait until nine — they open the next tab. An agent that answers on the spot is the only version of a reply that is always inside that window.

Both marks above point back to the numbered sources at the foot of Tentative business impact. This section adds no figure of its own to the ones cited there.
