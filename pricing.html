06 — Why Kalpataru Vista

The credits, the record, the close

Four practices are credited on the project — HB Design on architecture, Studio HBA on interiors, Burega Farnell on landscape, DJ Coalition on lighting — and each one swaps the photograph beside it when the visitor picks it. Under them the group's record counts up as it comes into view, and four benefits close the section.

The parts that don't have a picture

  • 01 — Show Apartment. A looping walkthrough of the apartment that is built and open to visit. It does not start downloading until it scrolls into view, so it never competes with the hero for bandwidth, and it swaps to a play control for anyone who has asked for reduced motion.
  • 07 — FAQs. Answers grouped by subject — the residences, location and connectivity, price and approvals, developer and design, visiting and downloads — filtered live as the visitor types, with a form for anything not covered.
  • The enquiry close. Book a site visit, download the plans, the address, the phone number, the email, and the RERA registration.
  • Two lead forms — the floor-plan download and the call-back request — each collecting name, number and intent, then opening a pre-filled email to the sales team.
  • A sticky enquire bar on the phone that appears once the hero is behind the visitor and stands down again when the enquiry section is on screen.
  • Link previews. Meta description, Open Graph and Twitter cards, so a link pasted into WhatsApp unfurls with the render and the copy rather than a bare URL.
  • Accessibility and restraint. The show-apartment video and the counters respect a reduced-motion preference, and every card that reacts to a mouse reacts to the keyboard too.
  • No dependencies to fail. The body face is self-hosted rather than fetched from a font service, and the site runs as flat files, so it can sit on any static host.

Not in this build: a sitemap, and any handoff from the enquiry forms into a CRM — today they open an email. Both sit in the scope priced further down.

Three ways to have this built

There are three honest options for a project like this, and two of them are not Dayam Insights. A template shop really is cheaper and really is faster. A full-service agency really does do more — brand strategy, photography, campaign management, an account team to call on a Sunday. What sits between them is senior craft at a price that is not carrying an agency's floor space.

Template shop, or a junior-heavy team on the same brief
₹ 80,000
Timeline

The fastest of the three, and not by a little. The theme already exists, so the work is configuration rather than construction.

Custom animation

Whatever the theme ships with. Scroll choreography is rarely in scope at this price, and retrofitting it into a bought theme usually costs more than building it once.

AI agent

Normally a third-party chat widget on its own subscription, answering from whatever is pasted into it rather than from the price sheet and the floor plates.

Code ownership

The content is yours. The theme and the platform are rented, and moving off them later means building the site a second time.

Genuinely the right call when the site is a placeholder, the budget is fixed, and nobody expects to touch it again.

The right call when the website is one deliverable inside a larger marketing mandate. If photography, media buying and an account manager are all needed, they should all be hired for.

The point is not that one of these three is correct. It is that the middle price is not an assertion: it is a published rate with a stated multiplier on it, and both halves are on this page.

What the research says, and does not say

Tentative business impact

Published benchmarks on how property enquiries are handled, and on what changes when the website answers them. Read them as the reason for the build, not as a forecast of it.

None of the figures below is a prediction about Kalpataru Vista, and nobody can honestly offer you one. They are industry-wide numbers about lead response, website chat and page speed, cited so you can read the study instead of taking the claim. What actually happens here will depend on your inventory, your pricing and your sales team. The site can only make sure the enquiry arrives quickly and arrives qualified.
